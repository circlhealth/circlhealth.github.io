How it works

Three simple steps.

No complicated setup. No medical jargon. Just clear, plain language — every time.

```
1
Record your visit

Open the app when your appointment begins. Let your doctor know you're recording. CirclHealth listens quietly in the background.

2
Get a clear transcript — instantly

When you're done, CirclHealth instantly turns what was said into a plain-language summary. No clinical jargon. Grade 6 reading level.

3
Understand what was said

Read it yourself, share it with family, or translate it for someone who needs it in their language. Your health information, clearly explained.

```

What we stand for

Built on four principles.

Clarity gives you the words. Agency gives you the voice. Trust opens the door. Community is where health thrives.

Clarity
Clarity gives you the words.

Medical language is designed for clinicians, not patients. We translate everything into plain, honest language that anyone can read and act on.

Agency
Agency gives you the voice.

Understanding your health gives you the ability to ask better questions, make better decisions, and be a more active participant in your own care.

Trust
Trust opens the door.

Your health information belongs to you — not to us, not to advertisers, not to researchers. CirclHealth will never use it without your explicit permission. Those decisions are yours to make and yours to change.

Community
Community is where health thrives.

The most effective care happens when patients, caregivers, and providers are working from the same information. My Circle exists because no one should navigate their health alone — this is the benefit of the circle of care.

The team
